## New features

- **Car evaluations of SOLECTRUS 1.4 on the stable channel.** The car sensors lose their "Beta" marker. The general settings ask for the country, and the dashboard settings offer the address lookup for car locations. With a car odometer or the heat of the heat pump, HELIOS adds the Public Data Collector. It fetches public fuel and gas prices for countries of the European Union. The new service stays stopped after the update and must be started on the Services screen.

## Improvements

- **A pending update of a service names its reason.** The tooltip of the update button lists the changed settings, for example a new sensor or a new version. For a change made before the update of HELIOS, the reason stays unknown.
